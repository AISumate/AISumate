# n8n.io Design Research

## Brand Colors
- **Primary Pink:** #EA4B71 (vibrant coral/pink)
- **Accent Dark:** #040506 (near-black)
- **Accent White:** #FFFFFF

## Additional Colors (from website observation)
- Dark backgrounds use deep navy/dark tones (#101330 from Brandfetch)
- Light backgrounds are clean white with subtle gray accents
- Pink is used as the primary accent/CTA color
- Cards and sections use subtle shadows and rounded corners

## Typography
- n8n uses a clean, modern sans-serif font
- Headings are bold and large
- Body text is readable and well-spaced
- The site uses Inter or similar geometric sans-serif

## Design Language
- Clean, spacious layouts with generous whitespace
- Rounded corners on cards and buttons
- Subtle shadows for depth
- Pink as the primary accent color for CTAs and highlights
- Dark mode with deep dark backgrounds and white text
- Light mode with white backgrounds and dark text
- Minimal borders, relying on shadows and spacing for separation
- Bold, confident headings with lighter body text

## Font Choice for aisumate
- **Inter** for body text (clean, modern, highly readable)
- **Space Grotesk** for headings (geometric, distinctive, tech-forward)
